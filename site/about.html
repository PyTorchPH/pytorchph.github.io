Mabuhay!

PyTorch Philippines is a nationwide, community-led network for people who learn, teach, research, and build with PyTorch and the broader machine learning ecosystem.

Join the community

Our mission

Make practical machine learning education and collaboration more accessible throughout the Philippines. We help people turn knowledge into responsible research, useful technology, open-source contributions, and measurable community impact.

What we do

  • Organize talks, workshops, study groups, and hands-on learning activities.
  • Support open-source projects, technical demonstrations, and educational resources.
  • Connect practitioners and researchers for mentorship, collaboration, and knowledge sharing.
  • Create opportunities for communities across the country to participate and lead.
  • Encourage responsible, reproducible, and inclusive machine learning practice.

Who can participate

PyTorch Philippines welcomes people at every experience level: first-time learners, educators, researchers, software and machine learning engineers, community organizers, and experienced contributors. Participation is open to anyone who wants to learn, share, build, mentor, or help strengthen the Philippine machine learning community. Respectful collaboration and meaningful contribution guide how we work together.

We are an independent user group. For official PyTorch news, releases, and documentation, visit pytorch.org.
