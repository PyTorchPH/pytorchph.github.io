PyTorch Philippines adopts the [PyTorch Foundation Code of Conduct]({{ site.external_urls.org_coc }}) for
all community spaces: events, online channels, the member portal, and repositories under
[PyTorchPH on GitHub]({{ site.external_urls.repo_org }}).

In short, we expect everyone to:

- Be welcoming and respectful to people of every background and experience level.
- Give and accept constructive feedback with empathy.
- Credit other people's work and respect privacy and consent.
- Keep discussions focused on learning, building, and helping each other.

Harassment, discrimination, and personal attacks are not tolerated in any form.

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the organizers
through the reporting form in the [member portal]({{ site.baseurl }}/portal/login/). Reports are reviewed
confidentially, and organizers may take any action they consider appropriate, up to removal from
community spaces.
