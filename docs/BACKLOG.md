## Parental contact for young members (not prioritized; added 2026-09-30)

Every age may join PyTorch Philippines (profile ages 1–120, migration 0017). Members under 18 —
especially young children — should have a parent or guardian the organization can reach.

- Onboarding asks members under 18 for a parent/guardian email or mobile number, and consent from
  that guardian before sensitive details (RA 10173: minors' data needs guardian consent).
- The backend queues guardian verification (email/SMS with a confirmation link or code), retries
  delivery, and records the outcome; officers see pending/confirmed status, never the contact
  itself unless their role needs it.
- Until confirmed, a young member's profile stays limited (for example: no public leaderboard name,
  no analytics consent, no event sign-ups that need guardian approval).
- Open questions: age threshold (under 13 vs under 18), SMS provider and cost, retention period,
  what an unconfirmed account can do.
