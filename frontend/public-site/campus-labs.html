What a Campus Lab does

A Campus Lab is a university-based group that brings classmates, educators, and researchers together around PyTorch, and links them to the rest of PyTorch Philippines.

Study together

Work through the learning path as a group, from tensors and training loops to ecosystem tools and deployment.

Build hands-on

Run workshops where members train real models, and turn class ideas into projects that work.

Share in the open

Publish code, notebooks, and write-ups so other students can learn from them and build on them.

Connect nationwide

Join community programs and meet PyTorch users from other universities across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

Grow your track record

Members can record their participation and build a résumé in the member portal.

For educators and researchers

The PyTorch Foundation runs an OSPO & Academic Outreach program that connects PyTorch with academia through a webinar series and hands-on teaching materials for educators, students, and researchers. It is run by the Foundation, not by PyTorch Philippines.

Start a Campus Lab

Any university in the Philippines can have a Campus Lab. You need a few people who want to learn PyTorch together.

  1. Create a member account

    Register in the member portal. Ask your co-organizers to register too.

  2. Form an organizing team

    Gather a few students who will run sessions with you, and a faculty adviser if your university requires one for student groups.

  3. Tell us about your plans

    {%- if contacts.email %}

    Email {{ contacts.email | escape }} with your university, your organizing team, and the sessions you want to run.

    {%- else %}

    Contact the organizers through the member portal with your university, your organizing team, and the sessions you want to run.

    {%- endif %}

  4. Run your first session

    Start with the first stage of the learning path and the sample code. Everyone follows the Code of Conduct.
