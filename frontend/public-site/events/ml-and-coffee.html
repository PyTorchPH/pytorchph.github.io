About ML & Coffee

ML & Coffee is PyTorch Philippines' meetup for people interested in machine learning, at any experience level. It is for meeting each other and sharing knowledge: short talks open the program, followed by a networking session.

  • 40 Attendees planned
  • 3 Talk levels
  • 5 Tracks

Overview

Talks are chosen from submitted proposals, which are grouped into three levels.

Levels

  • Ignite: beginner learning journeys and inspirations in machine learning.
  • Personal and fun projects: ML projects created without real-world or business constraints.
  • Industry and real-world impact: AI and ML implementations that highlight real case uses and their impact.
