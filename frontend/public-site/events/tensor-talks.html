About Tensor Talks

Tensor Talks is PyTorch Philippines' technical talk series on how modern AI systems are built: training, inference, the mathematics underneath, and agentic systems. It gives Filipino students and practitioners a recurring, technically deep venue, taught by local speakers.

Initialized is the first edition: an afternoon of full sessions and lightning talks, with the last hour for networking with the speakers. The series runs every two months and is run by community volunteers.

  • 1:00 to 6:00 PM Time
  • 12:30 PM Doors open
  • Free Admission
  • 60 Attendees planned

You also pick a level for your talk: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced.

Submission types

  • Session (20 to 30 minutes, up to 3 speakers): A focused talk covering a specific topic, concept, or case study, including Q&A.
    • Note: a talk with three or more speakers can't have only men on stage. This follows the Linux Foundation rule used at PyTorch events.
  • Lightning talk (10 to 15 minutes, up to 2 speakers): A fast-paced, high-impact presentation that delivers key insights or ideas in a concise format. There is no Q&A on stage; you take questions at the speaker corners during networking. A lightning talk is a good way to give your first technical talk.

The final length depends on how many talks we accept. Your acceptance email will state it.

Important notes

  • Submit up to two proposals. Each person can have at most one accepted talk.
  • This is a community event, so no product or vendor sales pitches.
  • Write the proposal yourself. Proposals that lean heavily on AI-generated or templated text rarely show the technical depth reviewers look for.
  • All speakers agree to follow the Linux Foundation Events Code of Conduct.
  • Strong proposals that don't fit January may be offered a slot at Tensor Talks in March 2027.
  • We take photos during the event. The submission form asks whether we may also record your talk.
  • Speakers receive a printed certificate.

How proposals are reviewed

A program committee scores every proposal with speaker names hidden, on relevance to PyTorch and ML practice, technical depth, clarity and originality.

It then builds the program from the top-scored proposals, balancing tracks and levels.
