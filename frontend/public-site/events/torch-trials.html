About Torch Trials

Torch Trials is PyTorch Philippines' ML-focused hackathon series. Instead of simply building an app, participants run experiments, test ideas, benchmark results, and present their findings, which makes it closer to a research paper hackathon than a traditional product-building competition.

It is a hands-on environment to explore core machine learning techniques through research-oriented challenges, to build the ability to design and analyze experiments, and to practice reproducible, evidence-driven problem solving.

  • 20 Professionals planned
  • 30 Students planned

Built around themes like

  • Model training
  • Inference
  • Speculative decoding
  • Quantization
  • Evaluation
