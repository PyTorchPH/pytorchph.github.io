{%- comment -%} The archipelago network (assets/images/ph-network.svg) with the three island groups labeled at the hubs the generator marks: Manila (Luzon), Cebu (Visayas), Davao (Mindanao). Positions are the hub coordinates as percentages of the 600 × 977 viewBox. With include.counts, each label also shows the number of published Campus Labs chapters for that island group (filled in by site-content.js). With include.filter, clicking a label filters the Campus Labs directory to that island group (assets/campus-directory.js); the island buttons there are the keyboard and screen reader route. {%- endcomment -%}
